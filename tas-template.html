Elevation: LIST Tasmania 25 m DEM (Land Tasmania, CC BY 3.0 AU; TIN of 1:25k 10 m contours, spot heights and water bodies; 90% within 5 m), exactly area-averaged to the chosen stud size. Blocks holding a LIST spot height take the surveyed value. Lakes: LIST Hydrographic Areas at full-supply level; a block is water when ≥50% covered. Sea = cells outside the DEM (heights are AHD, ≈ mean sea level). Grid is MGA zone 55. Labels from LIST Named Features and spot heights. Urban areas: LIST Landuse Live (ALUM 5.3–5.9 intensive uses excl. rural residential and recreation), shown as flat tiles where a block is ≥50% urban. Other surface colours are a slope/elevation classification, not mapped vegetation.

Sea-level rise is a static, uniform (“bathtub”) rise with hydraulic connectivity to the sea, computed on the metre heights before brick quantisation. It ignores ocean-dynamic and gravitational fingerprints, vertical land motion/GIA, tides and surge. The DEM interpolates between the 1 m coastline and the first 10 m contour, so rises of a few metres are not reliably resolved; use LiDAR for planning.
